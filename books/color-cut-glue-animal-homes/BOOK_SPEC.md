## 4. טקסט הכריכה האחורית (מוכן להעתקה)

```
🎨 ✂️ 📘  THREE BOOKS IN ONE

Color it. Cut it. Give every animal a home.

Bubi, Maya and Leo need your help! Twenty-four animals have lost their way
home — and only you can bring them back.

WHAT'S INSIDE
🎨  40 BIG & SIMPLE coloring pages — thick, bold lines made for little hands
✂️  24 cut-out animals to color, snip and keep
🏡  5 full-page habitat scenes — the Ocean, the Sky, the Farm,
     the Jungle and the Forest at Night
🏆  A finish-the-book certificate with your child's name

HOW IT WORKS
1. Color your favourite animal.
2. Cut it out along the dotted line (with a grown-up!).
3. Glue it where it belongs — the dolphin in the water,
   the bird in the sky, the cow on the farm.
4. Say it out loud: "The owl lives in the tree!"

WHY PARENTS LOVE IT
✔  SINGLE-SIDED PAGES — markers will never bleed onto the next picture
✔  Big 8.5" x 11" pages, one animal per page, no tiny details
✔  Builds scissor skills, pencil grip, and first science words
✔  Turns screen time into table time

Ages 3-6 · Preschool & Kindergarten · Use safety scissors with adult help

                                       🎁 Free bonus pages inside — see page 110
```
