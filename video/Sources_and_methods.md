Treasury yields are percentages, not bond prices. Moving averages: simple daily close averages. RSI/ATR: Wilder smoothing. CCI: 14-day typical-price mean deviation. Historical support/resistance references are not forecasts.

October statistics: 2011–2025 adjusted monthly close, September month-end to October month-end. Dividends follow Yahoo adjustments; this is a total-return proxy, not an independently constructed reinvested portfolio. The incomplete October 2026 is excluded.

News is attributed headline metadata, not independent full-article verification. Earnings from Nasdaq calendar are labelled estimated unless independently confirmed.
