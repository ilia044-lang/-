Treasury yields are percentages, not bond prices. Moving averages: simple daily close averages. RSI/ATR: Wilder smoothing. CCI: 14-day typical-price mean deviation. News is attributed headline metadata, not independent full-article verification. Earnings from Nasdaq calendar are labelled estimated unless independently confirmed.
