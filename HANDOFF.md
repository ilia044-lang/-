- **סדרת Trace & Color:** ספר עקיבת האותיות המוביל (Trace Letters, Sujatha Lalgudi, הוצאה עצמית) — כ-24K דירוגים; ספר Pencil Control של Wonder House — מקום 16 ברבי-המכר של Children's Activity Books, ~34K ביקורות. סדר הסדרה: קווים ← אותיות ← מספרים.

## נספח — כתב היד של Zoe (טיוטה, לפני עדכון פרק הבריכה)
## 2. Manuscript (read-aloud text)
Refrain: **"Stop. Look. Ask a grown-up!"**

**Spread 1** Zoe had a yellow star backpack, a puppy named Pickles, and one BIG wish.
**Spread 2** Ms. Rivera held up a shiny gold star. "On Friday it's Safety Star Day! Find five safety stars — at home, on the street, at school, at the pool, and from a real helper — and you'll be a Junior Safety Star!"
"I'll get ALL FIVE!" said Zoe. "Woof!" said Pickles. *(page turn)*

**HOME — Mom & Dad**
**Spread 3** Tug, tug, TUG! Pickles was pulling a lamp cord across the floor. The lamp wobbled.
Zoe stopped. She looked. "Stop. Look. Ask a grown-up!" "Dad! Pickles has the cord!"
**Spread 4** "Great stopping!" said Dad. "Cords are not for pulling. Only grown-ups plug in and unplug."
In the kitchen, Mom flipped pancakes. "The stove is a hot zone," said Mom. "Three big steps back."
One… two… THREE giant steps. Pickles took six tiny ones.
Mom stuck a gold star on Zoe's backpack. **One star!** *(page turn)*

**STREET — Officer Grant**
**Spread 5** On the way to school, white lights blinked on a car. Beep… beep… It was backing up!
"Stop!" said Zoe, holding Dad's hand tight. "White lights mean the car might move."
**Spread 6** At the corner stood Officer Grant with his big STOP sign. "Stop at the curb," he smiled. "Look left, look right, look left again. Walk, don't run."
Zoe looked left. Right. Left again. *Walk.*
**Spread 7** Bounce… bounce… Pickles' ball rolled toward the rushing drain! Pickles leaned in —
"STOP!" said Zoe. "Never go near a drain. The water is stronger than it looks."
**Spread 8** The ball splashed away. Pickles whimpered. "Balls can be replaced," said Officer Grant, kneeling down. "Puppies can't." He pinned a gold star on her backpack. **Two stars!** *(page turn)*

**SCHOOL — Ms. Rivera**
**Spread 9** BEEP! BEEP! BEEP! The fire alarm! Nobody ran. Nobody hid. Everyone walked out in a line to the big tree outside.
**Spread 10** At the gate, a man Zoe didn't know waved. "Come with me, I have a puppy too!"
Zoe stayed right where she was. "I stay with my grown-ups," she said, and ran to tell Ms. Rivera.
"That was exactly right," said Ms. Rivera. **Three stars!** *(page turn)*

**POOL — Coach Jay**
**Spread 11** After school, Zoe went to the pool with Mom. Pickles zoomed along the wet edge — slip, slide —
TWEET! went Coach Jay's whistle. "Walk, don't run!"
**Spread 12** "Water is for fun," said Coach Jay, "when a grown-up is watching, every single minute."
Zoe waved to Mom. Mom waved back. Watching. **Four stars!** *(page turn)*

**FIRST AID — Paramedic Sam (Zoe's aunt)**
**Spread 13** On Safety Star Day, Aunt Sam brought her ambulance to school. Then — oops! — Leo tripped and scraped his knee.
Zoe didn't panic. "Stop. Look. Ask a grown-up!" "Aunt Sam! Leo needs help!"
**Spread 14** Aunt Sam rinsed the scrape with clean water, pressed gently, and put on a bandage. "And if someone is badly hurt," she said, "we call 9-1-1 and say our name and address."
"My name is Zoe," said Zoe. "And I know my address."
Aunt Sam pinned on the last star. **FIVE stars!**
**Spread 15** "Zoe, you are a Junior Safety Star!" Everyone cheered. Pickles barked the loudest.
**p31** That night, Zoe counted her stars. One, two, three, four, five. "Stop. Look. Ask a grown-up," she whispered. Pickles was already asleep. **The End**
