# ACT III — THE PAYOFF — 3:00–3:32

The rules stop being a list and start mattering.

**S21 · 9s · WIDE** — Running to the swings, Leo trips on the grass and scrapes
his knee. He gets up and keeps playing quietly, glancing away. **No blood.**

**S22 · 8s · MEDIUM** — Bubi notices, comes over quickly, touches Leo's arm.
Bubi: *"Leo — you're hurt. That one's for a grown-up."*

**S23 · 8s · MEDIUM WIDE · MUSIC DROPS, THEN RESOLVES WARM** — Leo walks to Mom,
eyeline **up-left**. She kneels and looks at his knee.
Leo: *"I fell down."*
Mom: *"Thank you for telling me. That's exactly right."*

**S24 · 7s · WIDE** — Small clean bandage on. Leo runs back. All three play,
Mom in frame, full playground alive. Warm light.
Leo: *"Ready!"*
