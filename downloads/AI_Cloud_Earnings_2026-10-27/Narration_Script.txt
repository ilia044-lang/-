Can two earnings calls rescue five battered AI infrastructure stocks? Microsoft and Alphabet could change the mood. But more spending is not automatically more revenue for these companies.

The calendar lists Microsoft and Alphabet for Wednesday, October 28, after the U.S. close. Amazon follows Thursday. For Wednesday, focus on Azure and Google Cloud: growth, bookings, and what management says about future capacity.

Listen for three things: paid demand, capital spending, and delivery dates. Are customers actually using the capacity? Is new investment backed by cash flow? And when will new data centers start earning revenue? A bigger budget without better economics is not enough.

At Monday's close, all five stocks were below their twenty day moving averages. IREN, Cipher, CoreWeave and Nebius also had RSI below thirty. That signals weak recent momentum, not a confirmed bottom. These charts use the October twenty sixth close, not unfinished Tuesday trading.

CoreWeave and Nebius sell AI cloud computing. IREN combines data center infrastructure, AI cloud and bitcoin mining. Cipher and TeraWulf have exposure to power, data centers and hosting. More Big Tech spending could help demand, but building in house can bypass outside providers. A sector headline is not a signed contract.

A Yahoo Finance headline on October twenty sixth flagged rising costs to insure CoreWeave's debt. That puts funding risk beside the growth story. For these companies, watch debt, potential share dilution, customer concentration, and whether announced capacity gets delivered and paid for.

The constructive case is strong cloud demand, credible funding, and a price recovery with volume. The risk case is project delays, weaker growth, or spending that hurts cash flow. A bounce can happen while business risks remain. These are scenarios, not predictions.
