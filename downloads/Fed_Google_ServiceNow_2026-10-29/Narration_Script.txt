Google down about eleven percent. ServiceNow down about twenty. Is this a buying opportunity, or a warning? These are premarket moves, not closing prices.

Reports say the Fed held interest rates unchanged in a ten to two vote. That is a pause, not a promise of cuts. Stocks edged higher Wednesday before these earnings reactions. So don't blame both selloffs on the Fed alone.

For Alphabet, reporting points to missed earnings and revenue expectations, alongside concerns about AI investment and cash generation. Before calling the stock cheap, separate operating performance from investment related gains and losses. Then check free cash flow after capital spending.

ServiceNow's revenue reportedly beat expectations, but earnings missed, and the subscription outlook raised concerns. For a recurring revenue business, the next growth forecast matters. A lower share price is not automatically a better valuation if earnings expectations also fall.

Our October 29 snapshot is from seven eleven a.m. Eastern. The regular session has not opened. Watch whether price stops making new lows, reclaims a broken level, and holds with volume. A brief bounce alone does not confirm a bottom.

Today's calendar lists GDP, PCE inflation, and jobless claims at 8:30 ET. Amazon and Intel report after the close. These events can change the rates outlook and the earnings picture again. Don't treat an early quote as the final verdict.

For a short term setup, look for stabilization. For a long term investment, ask whether cash flow and growth justify the new price. Neither stock is a confirmed bargain simply because it fell. The decision needs evidence, not just a bigger discount.
