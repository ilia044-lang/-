TITLE
Dead Cat Bounce? Monday’s Rally: The Bull & Bear Case | Oct 19 #Shorts

DESCRIPTION
SPY and QQQ finished Monday higher—but both printed red daily candles. Was October 19, 2026 a dead cat bounce, or the beginning of a more durable recovery?

Bearish evidence: QQQ recovered only about 47% of Friday’s dollar loss; SPY and QQQ closed below their opens, near the bottom of their ranges, and below their 20- and 50-day averages. Equal-weight RSP fell 0.32% while QQQ rose 1.21%, suggesting uneven participation.

Constructive evidence: VIX fell from 19.99 to 18.44, Monday’s SPY/QQQ lows held above Friday’s lows, and both remained above their 200-day averages. QQQ volume was about 103% of the previous 20-session average, despite being lower than Friday.

QQQ reference levels: reclaim area 724.55–728.00; Monday low 716.57; Friday low 707.23. These are scenarios and historical references, not targets or predictions.

Conclusion: a possible relief bounce with weak participation. A confirmed dead cat bounce requires subsequent failure; Monday alone cannot establish that. Above the 200-day average does not guarantee a reversal either.
