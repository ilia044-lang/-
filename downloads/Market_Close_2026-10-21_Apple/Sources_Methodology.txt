MARKET MIND — SOURCES AND CALCULATIONS

Cutoffs: U.S. regular-session daily data through Wednesday October 21, 2026. News reviewed Thursday October 22, 2026 around 08:00 UTC. The oil quote is Reuters' explicitly dated 06:26 UTC snapshot (09:26 Israel time). No claim is made to cover the October 22 U.S. close; it had not happened when this video was produced.

Market source: Yahoo Finance chart endpoint, raw daily OHLCV, fetched October 22. Exact API URLs are in analysis.json; data used on screen are included in Evidence. Date uniqueness, ordering, bar high/low consistency, and exact final date were checked on retrieval. Daily percentage = (Oct 21 close / Oct 20 close - 1) × 100. Closing numbers are rounded only for presentation. Unadjusted closes are used for price changes and arithmetic SMA20/50. This is not a total-return calculation. ETFs are explicitly labelled; SPY is not the exact S&P 500 index and QQQ is not the Nasdaq Composite.

SPY closed above both SMA20 and SMA50. QQQ closed below both. The QQQ candle plot displays the last 20 actual daily sessions through October 21. The horizontal dashed line is the current 50-day average as a reference level, NOT a historical moving-average curve. Extruded candle front faces encode open/close; wicks encode high/low. Depth is a visual style only. Bar chart heights share zero and are linear in the labelled daily returns. VIX bars also share zero; changing perspective does not change encoded height. Closing brand bars are decorative, not data.

RSP is an equal-weight fund used as a participation proxy, not an advancing-stock count. “Technology leading these benchmarks” describes QQQ's stronger return among the displayed comparisons; it is not a claim that every technology stock rose. VIX refers to annualized expected roughly 30-day S&P 500 volatility inferred from options; lower VIX is not a guarantee of safety or a forecast of positive returns.

The video attributes the reported attacks and U.S. family-withdrawal plans to Reuters and its stated sources. Unverified IRGC shipping claims and explosions of unknown origin are omitted. Iran/U.S./Israel appears as a forward-looking monitoring list; the video does not assert a newly verified Israeli strike. Statements about what might happen next are not predictions.

Raw continuous WTI futures data were collected during research but are not used to calculate the on-screen overnight return; continuous futures can change contracts. The displayed +2.31% is Reuters' same-story quote. This prevents a roll-sensitive historical series from being confused with a same-contract settlement return. Unused macro fields remain in analysis.json for research context.
