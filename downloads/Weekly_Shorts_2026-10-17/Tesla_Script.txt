Tesla made a car do this. But what does the demonstration actually prove?

This is the Roadster event recording supplied for our weekly review. Reuters reported rocket style thrusters developed with SpaceX. Tesla describes cold gas thrusters, active aerodynamics, and a suction system. These are attributed descriptions, not our independent engineering tests.

Gas pushed out creates force in the opposite direction. Fans removing air from beneath the car can add downforce when the sealing system works. Thrusters placed around a vehicle can also create rotation.

The important distinction: a dramatic demonstration does not establish public road approval, production scale, or future earnings. Tesla lists restrictions for the specialized configuration. Manufacturer performance figures remain claims until independently tested.
