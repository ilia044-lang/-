Extreme fear. Guaranteed rebound? Not so fast. The historical comparison changes the story.

On October sixteenth, CNN's Fear and Greed index hit seventeen point nine four. The V I X jumped twenty one point four five percent that Friday. But fear alone does not prove the selling is finished.

We checked fifteen years for days when S P Y fell at least one percent and V I X rose at least twenty percent. Across seventy four completed events, S P Y was higher twenty trading sessions later about seventy percent of the time.

The ordinary market baseline was already about sixty nine percent. That is only a small difference. Events can cluster, and the worst twenty session outcome in the event sample was a loss of about thirty percent.
