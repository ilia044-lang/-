Amazon has a big cloud demand story, but its short term chart still needs repair. The calendar lists earnings Thursday after the close.

At the October 23 close, Amazon was at $248. The weekly low at $246.45 is a support reference. Resistance is near $259.49. Price is below its twenty and fifty day averages, but above the two hundred day line. That longer term average is another nearby reference.

RSI is 43.2. Friday traded below its opening price and finished in the lower half of its range. Average true range is about $6.66 a day. It describes recent movement, not a limit on an earnings gap.

Recent coverage quotes Andy Jassy saying large blocks of 2027 AWS capacity are largely booked. Those commitments are not revenue already earned. Reported sales guidance is $197B–$202B. Watch AWS growth against roughly $220B in annual capital spending.

The earnings estimate is $1.96 per share. The October 30 at-the-money straddle midpoint gives a rough 7.8 percent move in either direction. It covers risk through expiry, not just earnings, and the actual move can be larger.

Holding $246.45 and reclaiming $259.49 would improve the chart. A break below support would weaken it. Watch the reaction and volume after the report. These are scenarios, not predictions.
