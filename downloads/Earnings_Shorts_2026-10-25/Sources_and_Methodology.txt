SMA20/50/150/200 use closes through Oct23. RSI14 and ATR14 use Wilder smoothing. CCI14 uses typical price and its 14-bar mean absolute deviation. Volume ratio includes Friday in the 20-session average. Daily ATR is a historical range statistic, not a cap on an earnings gap.
Support is explicitly a reference at the Oct19–23 weekly low, not a proven multi-touch floor. Resistance uses the closer of the weekly high or a confirmed 3-bar-on-each-side swing high above the close from the preceding 65 sessions. Prior swing points must have right-side bars, avoiding unconfirmed future pivots. Conditional paths are illustrative and are not targets. Numeric labels are outside the plot. Charts smoothly zoom from 50 to 25 daily bars; moving averages outside the visible price window remain listed numerically. Solid dimensional candle bodies represent actual OHLC; depth is decorative.

OPTIONS
Same stock, Oct30 expiry and identical call/put strike nearest the stock close. Selected strike within 2.5% of spot. All legs have positive, non-crossed bid/ask snapshots. Underlying regular-market timestamps are Oct23; contracts' last trades are also Oct23, but some last trades are stale/asynchronous. We use bid/ask midpoints, NOT last-trade prices, for the video estimates.
Formula: [(call bid + call ask)/2 + (put bid + put ask)/2] / stock close × 100. No 0.85 adjustment is applied. Dollar references are stock close ± combined premium, not exact straddle breakevens, which would be centered on the strike. This is an approximate premium-based movement yardstick for all risk through expiry, not an isolated earnings move, model confidence interval, direction signal or maximum possible loss/move.
Quotes were retrieved Oct25 during the weekend. Per-quote exchange timestamps are not supplied, so these are not certified simultaneous closing quotes and are not claimed to be live or executable. Liquidity, stale quotes, spreads and fees can change the measure. LLY, CAT and MA have combined bid/ask widths over 15% of combined midpoint and get an additional on-screen/spoken wide-spread note. No options trade is recommended; a buyer can lose the full premium and uncovered selling can involve very large losses.
