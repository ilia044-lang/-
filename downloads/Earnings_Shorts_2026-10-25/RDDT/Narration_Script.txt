Reddit is testing its weekly low. Can advertising growth outweigh user growth concerns? The calendar lists earnings Thursday after the close.

At the October 23 close, Reddit was at $144.25. The weekly low at $143.92 is a support reference. Resistance is near $160.41. Price sits below all four moving averages. RSI remains below fifty, so momentum has not confirmed a recovery.

RSI is 41.1. Friday closed near the bottom of its range. That is weakness, not reversal confirmation. Average true range is about $7.65 a day. It describes recent movement, not a limit on an earnings gap.

The earnings preview reports company revenue guidance of $860M–$870M. Stronger advertising revenue per user has helped growth, while search referrals and user expansion remain risks. Watch whether better monetization can continue without relying on a rapid increase in the audience.

The earnings estimate is $1.32 per share. The October 30 at-the-money straddle midpoint gives a rough 12.9 percent move in either direction. It covers risk through expiry, not just earnings, and the actual move can be larger.

Holding $143.92 and reclaiming $160.41 would improve the chart. A break below support would weaken it. Watch the reaction and volume after the report. These are scenarios, not predictions.
