IRAN / MARKET SCENARIO STUDY — NOVEMBER 3, 2026

QUESTION AND SCOPE
How did traded proxies react around selected prior Israel/US-Iran escalations, how quickly did broad-equity closing prices regain the pre-event baseline, and which economic channels might matter in a renewed or broader strike? This is a conditional scenario framework. It does not verify a new attack decision, predict timing, assign attack probabilities or recommend a personal trade.

DATA
28 daily traded/index proxies: SPY, QQQ, RSP, IWM, Dow, VIX, US10yr yield, WTI, Brent, dollar index, BTC/USD, GLD, TLT, HYG, XLE, ITA, JETS, SOXX and the remaining major S&P sector ETFs. Yahoo chart API range10y/interval1d, retrieved Nov3, filtered to completed Nov2 dates. Equity dates are NY exchange dates; Bitcoin dates are UTC. See analysis.json for retrieval time and source URLs. Historical CSVs and raw responses are retained in Evidence. The complete 28-asset snapshot and 140-event/asset table accompany the video. Not every instrument or global market is represented.

EVENT SELECTION AND LIMITS
Predefined illustrative case list: Jan3,2020 US strike on Soleimani in Iraq; Apr19,2024 Israeli strike in Iran; Oct26,2024 Israeli weekend strikes (US sessionOct28); Jun13,2025 Israeli opening strikes; Jun22,2025 US nuclear-facility strikes (US sessionJun23). These dates are established historical context used to define windows, not a fresh independent audit of military primary records. The list is not an exhaustive event database; selection bias is possible. January2020 is outside Iranian territory. June2025 windows overlap and cannot be treated as independent trials. 2026 cached reporting describes existing conflict, so the present video concerns renewed/broader escalation, not the first-ever hostilities.

RETURN AND RECOVERY DEFINITIONS
US market baseline = close immediately preceding the first affected cash session. Day1,Day5,Day20 = nth observed trading-session close divided by baseline minus1. First regain = first close at/above baseline within61 observations (including event day), reported as elapsed sessions AFTER the event session. Thus zero = same session, not a future rebound forecast. Two-close confirmation = the second of the first consecutive pair at/above baseline. Checks also record whether price fell below baseline again within5 observations of first regain. These are closing-price measurements, not intraday recovery, all-time-high recovery, a causal event effect or a portfolio return. Equity dividends/distributions are excluded; adjusted-close sensitivity fields are retained. None of the displayed SPY/QQQ first-regain dates changed in the adjusted-close check. First5-session closing and intraday lows are supplementary fields; their windows are not a calibrated downside forecast.

Bitcoin uses calendar days and the commonly referenced Middle East event date. First reports can occur on a different UTC/US date, so the daily bars may straddle news. The video only describes the June22 close and June23 rebound; it does not call the daily loss the full attack drawdown. Do not compare a5-calendar-day BTC result as if it were5 US trading sessions. Futures series are front-month/continuous quotes with potential roll discontinuities, not total returns. Historical oil comparisons in the video use Brent, and current WTI is a price level. A revised Oct30 Brent bar differed from an older saved snapshot, so no current Brent daily return is used in narration. Source-vintage changes are not silently merged. Index volatility and Treasury-yield series are levels; their percent changes are not investor returns.

CONFOUNDERS
January2020's later window includes emerging pandemic risk. October2024's later window includes US election developments. June2025 includes evolving retaliation/ceasefire expectations and earnings/macro news. There is no control group, causal model, statistically valid mean recovery time or calibrated probability. A green day can reflect an outcome being less damaging than feared, but the price observation alone cannot prove that explanation. The video gives possible transmission mechanisms separately from measured returns. HYG price is not a credit spread; GLD and TLT are different exposures; ITA includes civilian aerospace businesses.

Signed bars use proportional heights and a sharedzero. Recovery plots use a normalized100 baseline; replay markers show historical observations only.
