A reported $6.3 billion Binance heist. Does that mean Bitcoin itself was hacked, or everyone should sell? Those are different questions.

Reuters and the Financial Times reported the theft. That does not mean all stolen assets were Bitcoin, or that Bitcoin's underlying network was breached. The attack details and recoveries are not independently verified here.

Custody risk is where your assets are held. Price risk is what they are worth. Check official notices through trusted channels, and avoid recovery scams.
